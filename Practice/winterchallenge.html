2026 Winter Practice Challenges:

Below, you can find three kinds of practice challenges. You may complete more than one challenge in one round or sitting. You are encouraged to repeat challenges for practice, but you may only earn points from each once.

Challenge Milestones:

  • 6 Points: You are automatically fully qualified through the end of the semester.
  • 10 points: You earn one Group Point for your group.
  • 20 points: You earn two Group Points for your group.
  • 30 points: You earn four Group Points for your group.

Beauty Challenge

POLISHED PREP

+2

Prep a polished round, where each contention flows logically, is correctly supported, and has no error in links and impacts. You may expand your prep time to an hour.

Submit: Image File

POLISHED SPEECH

+2

Present a polished speech from a round. Film one speech from a round (It does not have to be an ongoing round) and watch the recording. Deliver the speech again and record it. Repeat until your speech is rhetorically polished and excellent. (Do not exceed 4 iterations).

Submit: Video File

POLISHED FLOW

+2

Create a complete flow from a round with annotations. Track every argument. Make every connection, link, and impact explicit. Put comments on every argument. Evaluate every point against the weighing mechanism.

Submit: Image File
